How it works

Try a tool. Keep it. Make it yours.

  1. 1

    Preview official examples for free

    Open Discover to explore Photo Wall, Voice Note, and Leave Reminder before signing in. AI actions require a connected ChatGPT account.

  2. 2

    Tap Use This to keep one

    Save your own copy, then open it again from Apps inside Leafling.

  3. 3

    Describe what you want to create or change

    Connect an existing ChatGPT account to create a personal web app or edit one through chat. Generated apps run inside Leafling, not as standalone App Store apps. Your ChatGPT account usage limits apply.

You choose when to use device features.

Photos, microphone, location, calendar, and other sensitive features are requested after you tap an action that needs them. You control permission through iOS.
