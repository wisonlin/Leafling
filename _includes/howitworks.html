How it works

Try Photo Wall first. Keep what feels useful. Create when you are ready.

  1. 1

    Preview official examples for free

    Open Leafling and try Photo Wall before connecting an account or creating your own version.

  2. 2

    Tap Use This to keep one

    Photo Wall becomes your local tiny app so you can open it again from Apps or improve it in its related chat.

  3. 3

    Describe your own version

    Connect an existing ChatGPT account when you want Leafling to turn the example into your own photo helper.

Native access stays user-triggered.

Generated apps do not silently read your photos, location, contacts, calendar, microphone, or reminders. Sensitive iPhone abilities are requested from explicit actions inside the app.
