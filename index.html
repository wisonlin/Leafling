Make the apps you want on your iPhone.

Describe a personal tool in chat. Open it in Leafling, find it again in Apps, and ask for changes as you use it.

For iPhone and iPad. Try official examples before connecting AI.

From an idea to a tool you can reopen.

  1. Ask for one app.

    Start with a job you do often. Describe the screen and what you need it to do.

  2. Open the saved result.

    Use the app card in chat, then check that the buttons and results work for you.

  3. Use it from Apps.

    Find the same tool by its icon and reopen it. To change it, return to its chat.

Example shown with sample data. AI chat and edits need internet and an AI connection in Settings.
