A voice checklist,
made your way.

Design concept. This proposed end-to-end flow has not yet been tested.

Example request“Make me a voice checklist app. Let me choose which items to add to Reminders.”
  1. 01

    Make your app in chat

    Describe the tool you want to use. The concept starts with a personal voice checklist that opens inside Leafling.

  2. 02

    Speak, then review

    Tap Record and allow microphone and speech recognition access. Review the transcript and choose the tasks to keep.

  3. 03

    Add to Reminders

    Confirm with Add selected to Reminders. With your permission, the chosen items go into the iPhone Reminders app, where you can check them.

  4. 04

    Keep changing your app

    Return to chat: “Separate work and personal tasks.” Change the categories inside your own app, then keep using the version that fits you.

How does Leafling connect to iPhone features?

Personal apps use HTML, CSS, and JavaScript inside Leafling. A JSON Bridge connects supported actions to native iOS SDKs. Device availability and permissions determine which features an app can use. Your personal apps run inside Leafling.
