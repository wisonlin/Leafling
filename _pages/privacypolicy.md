# Privacy Policy

Leafling helps you create, run, and improve personal apps through chat on your iPhone or iPad.

## Information You Provide

Leafling may store your conversations, drafts, prompts, generated app files, version history, ChatGPT account status, and model preferences locally on your device.

ChatGPT OAuth tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. Basic account metadata and model preferences are stored locally on your device.

## AI Requests

When you send an AI message, Leafling sends your message and relevant conversation context to ChatGPT to produce a reply. If you ask it to create or edit an app, relevant project files may also be sent. When you explicitly use an AI feature inside an example or saved app, the content you choose to process may be sent to ChatGPT. Unsent drafts are stored locally and are not sent as AI requests.

Leafling does not require you to paste an API key, base URL, or provider configuration in the current version. ChatGPT sign-in is managed through a secure web sign-in flow.

## Local Project Storage

Conversations, drafts, saved apps, and version history are stored locally on your device. Leafling does not publish your generated apps to a public marketplace or server.

## Optional iCloud Key-Value Storage

Some personal apps can use Leafling's iCloud key-value bridge to store text values in the iCloud account signed in on the device. When you choose a feature that uses this bridge, those values may be synchronized by Apple to your other devices with iCloud enabled. This does not automatically sync your Leafling conversations, app files, or version history.

Cloud storage is separate from local project storage and is not a public app listing. Availability depends on your iCloud account and device settings.

## Device Permissions

Some built-in examples or generated projects may request iOS permissions, such as camera, photo library, microphone, speech recognition, location, calendar, reminders, motion, or Face ID access.

Permission prompts are shown by iOS and are controlled by you. Leafling requests these permissions only when you interact with a feature that needs them.

Photo-library queries follow your iOS authorization. Choosing an item through the system photo picker provides that selected content; it does not grant full-library access.

## Motion & Fitness Data

Leafling may read step count or distance estimates from the device pedometer only after you explicitly interact with an example or generated project that requests Motion & Fitness access.

Personal apps may also use supported accelerometer, gyroscope, orientation, or battery updates. Continuous subscriptions operate while the personal app is open and Leafling is in the foreground. They stop when you leave the app page or put Leafling in the background; they do not restart automatically.

Leafling does not use Motion & Fitness data for advertising, analytics, tracking, or sale.

## Usage Analytics

Leafling may send limited usage analytics to PostHog Cloud to help us understand whether core flows are working. These events can include app launch, foreground, tab selection, onboarding, example preview, Use This, project creation, chat submission status, agent run result, app open, app share, `.leafling` import result, key Bridge API outcome, ChatGPT sign-in outcome, model tier selection, paywall, and purchase or restore results.

Usage analytics use an anonymous install identifier and basic technical context such as app version, build number, device model, OS version, locale, and time zone. Leafling does not send prompts, project names, generated app files, ChatGPT emails, OAuth tokens, Bridge parameters, photos, contacts, calendar data, location, or other permission payloads in usage analytics.

## Website Analytics

On this website, optional analytics run only after you choose **Allow analytics**. You can refuse or withdraw through **Analytics choices**. We also respect Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control, and an explicit `analytics=off` link option. Refusing does not block downloads or other site features.

With your permission, we send page-visit and App Store-link click events to PostHog Cloud. Each event contains only an approved page path, predefined channel/campaign/content labels, the click location, an internal-test flag, a timestamp, and a random identifier held in memory for that page visit. We store only your analytics preference in this browser; we do not store a visitor identifier, set analytics cookies, fingerprint your device, record sessions, or automatically capture other interactions. Counts therefore describe opted-in events and page visits, not unique people.

We do not send full URLs, query strings, referrers, free-form tracking values, account details, or page content as analytics properties. Network requests necessarily reach PostHog with network information; we disable geographic enrichment and person-profile creation and do not use network information to match website visits to app installations.

Approved App Store links may include Apple's provider and campaign labels after you allow analytics. Apple's aggregate campaign reports are separate from website counts and anonymous iOS usage events. We do not join them into individual journeys or infer which person installed or purchased the app. Earlier links without these labels cannot be attributed retroactively.

## Tracking and Advertising

Leafling does not include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking in the current version.
